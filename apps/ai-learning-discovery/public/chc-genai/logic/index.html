    Final · Agreed survey structure

    Choose → rate → share what matters.

    Staff choose the most relevant learning level, rate topics within that level, then tell us where AI is helping or could help. No scoring matrix or automatic recommendation.

    3 questions
    Q1

    Which training level interests you?

    Choose Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced. The topics for each level are visible before choosing.

    Q2

    How interested are you in each topic?

    Rate only the topics under your chosen level. The other levels stay hidden.

    Formstack conditional logic: Q1 controls which topic-rating questions appear in Q2. A suggested simple scale is Not interested / Interested / Very interested.

    Q3

    How could AI make a difference in your work?

    Four open-ended questions for all participants, regardless of their selected level.

    Preview only: This page does not submit, save or share answers. HR will recreate the agreed questions in Formstack.
