    The design decision

    Keep the matrix. Improve what the answers tell us.

    We are not changing how a level is suggested. We are changing the topics from a collection of AI features into distinct decisions and capabilities: what to delegate, how to verify it, and when a person must remain in charge.

    For recreating this in Formstack

    Use a lookup table for Steps 1–2. Show the suggested result in Step 3, allow staff to override it, then branch directly to that level's Step 4 topic list. The After tab shows the recommended CHC lists.
