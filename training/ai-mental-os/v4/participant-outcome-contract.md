# The one-page Outcome Contract

**The paid training's take-home artifact.** Start with the person and the useful result, not a prompt.

| Field | Question | Your answer |
|---|---|---|
| Human result | Whose work or life should improve? | ______________________________ |
| Deliverable | What exactly should exist when this is done? | ______________________________ |
| Source | What approved facts or files may AI use? | ______________________________ |
| Proof | What 2–3 checks could show whether it worked? | ______________________________ |
| Negative case | What is one thing that must **not** happen? | ______________________________ |
| Limits | What may AI fix alone, and when must it stop? | ______________________________ |
| Handoff | What result, evidence and exceptions do I need? | ______________________________ |

## Five-minute partner test

- Can your partner recognize the desired result without asking you to explain it again?
- Could a real check detect at least one mistake?
- Does the contract keep external, sensitive or irreversible actions behind an appropriate approval gate?

**Useful request format:** “Using [source], produce [deliverable] for [human outcome]. It passes when [checks]. Never [forbidden action]. Attempt routine corrections within [limit], then give me PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN and the relevant evidence.”

**Reminder:** A model's self-review is not independent proof. Use source checks, known-answer fixtures or human judgment appropriate to the risk.
