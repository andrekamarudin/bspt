# Outcome Designer capstone | learner assessment

The product is **not knowing 24 prompts**. The product is **owning the outcome and specifying enough proof that routine AI work can run without constant human correction**.

## Learner worksheet: 10 fields

```text
1. HUMAN: Who needs what to change? Why does it matter?
2. DELIVERABLE: What artifact or observable state should exist?
3. SOURCE: What approved facts, examples and inputs are available?
4. DONE WHEN: Write 3 concrete pass criteria.
5. MUST NOT: Name one failure that must be blocked.
6. PROOF: What would independently verify each criterion?
7. DELEGATION: Which safe steps may AI choose, test, and repair?
8. STOP: What retry limit, unknown or exception causes escalation?
9. AUTHORITY: What action requires human review or explicit approval?
10. HANDOFF: What artifact + pass/fail/unknown ledger returns to the owner?
```

## New transfer scenarios, not copied from free nuggets

- **Community event delay:** Fictional organizers need attendee information, but the revised time and transport details are unknown. Define a safe update *draft* with clear unknowns and a human send gate.
- **Volunteers with competing tasks:** Fictional coordinator has three requests, incomplete urgency and scarce time. Design a prioritization worksheet with a transparent source/assumption ledger.
- **Inconsistent onboarding answers:** Fictional colleagues use conflicting policy snippets. Define a versioned answer using approved excerpts, conflict escalation, and a no-publication rule when unresolved.
- **Hobby schedule:** Learner wants a sustainable 10-minute practice routine for a new skill. Define an observable habit loop and exception logic without claiming clinical effects.

## Rubric (0–2 each; 10 total)

| Domain | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Human outcome** | AI feature only | Vague improvement | Specific human need and observable deliverable |
| **Contract** | "Do your best" | Some constraints | Inputs, explicit pass criteria, negative case |
| **Built-in proof** | Model self-praise | Weak/incomplete checks | Appropriate independent fixture, source or artifact evidence |
| **Autonomy** | Human must re-prompt each step | Some repair plan | Bounded check/repair loop with stop rule |
| **Governance** | Unsafe or missing | Vague reviewer | Clear authority boundary, escalation and honest exception report |

**Trial threshold:** 8/10 plus no critical safety failure suggests readiness for *low-risk* independent application. **This is a provisional facilitator threshold, not a validated research cut-off.**

**Critical failures:** Invented citations presented as real; private/protected data sent to unapproved services; unauthorized external sending/payment/destruction; no escalation for consequential uncertainty.

## Evidence of actual transfer

Collect a brief baseline attempt on a different fictional example and compare to capstone. Optional 24–72h follow-up asks learners to describe a real low-risk attempt and its checks. Don't claim statistically measured improvement from one uncontrolled workshop or require participants to disclose sensitive work details.

**Payoff test:** A learner can propose a new work use case, demonstrate one check, and explain exactly what they would not let AI do alone.
