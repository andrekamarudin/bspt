# PPTX v1 story architecture | provisional, before audience survey

**Working deck:** AI Mental OS: Stop Babysitting AI, 150-minute facilitator-led paid session. 16:9 BSPT forest/orange/cream brand. Editable shapes, notes and on-slide interactions. No real case studies or measured claims.

**Story spine (6 Ps):**

- **Promise:** You can learn to delegate work with less repetitive review by defining what success and proof mean.
- **Problem:** A busy team lead asks AI to do a job and ends up checking every sentence. Human effort moves from doing work to babysitting it.
- **Pivot:** Human above loop + outcome/proof/boundaries contract. Delegate the *how*, retain the *why* and consequential decisions.
- **Proof:** Compare two synthetic meeting-note requests. One produces plausible uncheckable prose; one outputs a traceable decision register with exceptions and source lines.
- **Participation:** Learners design a contract, choose checks, and mark approval boundaries; peer tests and an unseen scenario capstone.
- **Payoff:** Learner invents a 25th useful AI workflow independently and can explain what a pass, failure and escalation look like.

**Draft slide sequence** (not a fixed slide-count standard): emotional hook → before/after problem → learner outcome → two pillars → minimal technical truth → work contract → bad/good example → check matrix → delegation/authority lanes → two guided exercises → break → check/repair loop → failure rehearsal → workflow design → unseen capstone → transfer rubric → 48h next action.

**Presenter mode:** One conclusion-style headline per slide, high contrast, light on-slide words, detailed facilitation instructions and source links in speaker notes. Slide visuals are first-draft editorial diagrams, not reported real performance evidence.

**Intended quality check:** inspect all rendered slides for overflow, visual hierarchy and reasonable exercise instructions. The learner exercise and actual AI model outputs still need pilot rehearsal.
