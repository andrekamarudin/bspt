# AI Mental OS: v2 visual storyboard

**Status:** A 26-slide, editable 16:9 PowerPoint redesign was generated and QA-reviewed on 8 November 2026. Facilitator notes total 150 minutes including a break. No audience survey results or measured efficacy claims.

**Human story:** Move the learner from 'I babysit AI' to 'I direct AI'. The free nugget vault provides use cases; the paid training develops the ability to invent the 25th independently.

**Two governing principles:** Human above the loop, with precise human authority for sensitive actions. Outcome + Proof + Boundaries, defined before the AI begins, so execution is checkable with less routine human vetting.

## Six visual jobs, not one repeated layout

1. Human moment: fictional Mei's frustration in an AI-generated office image.
2. Contrast: human operator versus architect; vague request versus checkable brief.
3. Mechanism: model/context/tools/checks, above-loop feedback, approval boundary.
4. Evidence: fictional source snippets linked to classifications and receipts.
5. Practice: baseline task, 18-minute contract exercise and capstone.
6. Release: a reflective break, the 25th nugget, and the closing statement.

## Six-P story functions: quality check

- **Promise:** competence to delegate by outcomes, not memorized prompts.
- **Problem:** fictional Mei must recheck an AI summary that invents commitments.
- **Pivot:** human above the loop plus success provable by design.
- **Proof:** trace source lines to proposed, action and unknown states; show real checks and honest limitations.
- **Participation:** baseline reflection, contract writing, peer challenge and novel capstone.
- **Payoff:** learner can invent and defend the 25th AI-assisted workflow.
