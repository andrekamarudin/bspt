# BSPT AI Mental OS v3: a visual thread, not a repeated template

**Deck delivered:** 26 editable PPTX slides, 150-minute workshop and speaker notes, with the same paid curriculum as v2. The PowerPoint and rebuildable source zip are attached to this conversation; GitHub stores the design and curriculum decisions.

## Continuity system

- **Authentic BSPT signature:** original-derived double brush geometry in every standard slide header and on cover and payoff. [Source SVG](../../logos/brush-slash.svg).
- **Five chapter markers:** Human Problem, Mental Shift, Define Done, Verify + Delegate, Your Own Use Case.
- **Progression:** a continuous coral footer track increases through all 26 slides to signal momentum.
- **Actual field notes:** taped notebook pages, ruled margin, paperclip, handwritten notes and yellow marker highlights on relevant evidence and activity slides. Original [paperclip](../../motifs/paperclip.svg), [swash](../../motifs/marker-swash.svg) and [semantic arrow](../../motifs/hand-arrow.svg) geometry retained.
- **Sparing motion:** two non-looping, 31-frame animated brush GIFs on slides 23 and 25, where the learner's independently designed 25th use case and achievement are revealed.
- **Useful diversity:** slides still vary their composition by human moment, contrast, mechanism, evidence, practice and release.

## Motion and fidelity

The PPTX GIF was generated from the previous source-derived transparent double-brush silhouette to be compatible with PowerPoint; **it is not an embedded copy of the repo's original motion GIF**. Final orange silhouette uses the official geometry. The complete texture and fine-grain detail from the original 46KB SVG are not yet fully represented in that raster. The original [animated SVG](../../motion/brush-slash-draw.svg) and [transparent GIF](../../motion/brush-slash-draw-transparent.gif) remain the source assets for later refinement.

**Evidence:** all 26 slides rendered and visually reviewed through LibreOffice; structural overflow passed; all 26 speaker-note sections preserved; both GIF assets are still multiframe and have no repeat loop. **Native PowerPoint slideshow motion has not been tested** and still needs verification on the presentation computer. A PNG/PDF preview usually shows only the first frame.

## Brand rule going forward

Build recognition through recurring signature + chapter/progress + occasional authentic hand-marked field notes, **not through imposing the same card layout on every slide**. Only place arrows where their endpoint identifies a real claim. Use handwriting and highlights to emphasize actual learning, not decorate empty corners.

See [Figma brand specimens](https://www.figma.com/design/hpKg4rZiyQJYwQ7TVBAnft), [canonical tokens](../../brand/tokens.json), and [v2 storyboard](slide-storyboard.md). No learner feedback or measured training outcomes are claimed.
