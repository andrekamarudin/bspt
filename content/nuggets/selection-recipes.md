# Build your own workshop from nuggets

These are **option pools**, not decks, chronological sequences or survey results. Select by an actual audience problem and facilitate each unit as human situation → demonstration → learner attempt → proof of learning.

## Confidence starter (potentially suitable for any beginner)
- [001](cards/BSPT-001.md) Teach it again differently: nobody needs to pretend to understand.
- [002](cards/BSPT-002.md) Let AI ask what is missing before it answers.
- [005](cards/BSPT-005.md) Replace passive watching with a micro-challenge.
- [016](cards/BSPT-016.md) Test the confident AI answer before trusting it.

**Change we seek:** “AI is approachable, useful, and I can check it.”

## Emotional hooks without manufactured drama
- Feeling **left behind** → [001](cards/BSPT-001.md), [005](cards/BSPT-005.md), [024](cards/BSPT-024.md): competence and hope.
- Feeling **overloaded** → [002](cards/BSPT-002.md), [007](cards/BSPT-007.md), [010](cards/BSPT-010.md): relief and control.
- Feeling **exposed to mistakes** → [014](cards/BSPT-014.md), [016](cards/BSPT-016.md), [023](cards/BSPT-023.md): safety and agency.
- Feeling **unable to build** → [020](cards/BSPT-020.md), [021](cards/BSPT-021.md), [022](cards/BSPT-022.md): pride and mastery.

## Rules for final selection

- Don't assign a nugget solely by job title; ask what people actually struggle with.
- Avoid six near-identical prompting lessons. Combine distinct outcomes and tools.
- Never force one 10-minute exercise into a 90-second live demonstration.
- For mixed audiences, choose provider-agnostic text-only exercises as the baseline. Test all browser/AI access beforehand.
- Once survey and pilot feedback exist, record actual interest, observed success, confusion and real requests. Re-rank **then**, not now.
