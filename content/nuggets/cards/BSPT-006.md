# Turn a foggy topic into the right questions

> **Tell a friend:** First find the question worth answering.

**Human problem:** You want to learn about AI agents, insurance or a new system but don't know which question to ask first.

**Byte-size ProTip:** Ask AI to map unknowns by what matters to your goal, then choose one discriminating next question.

## The before → after moment

**Safe fictional setup:** Goal: 'I want to know whether our team should pilot an AI agent for repetitive admin work.'

**Copy/paste demo prompt** (works in an ordinary text chat):

```text
I'm exploring whether our team should pilot an AI agent for repetitive admin work. I have no access to internal metrics yet. Give me FIVE practical questions in order of decision value, explaining in one line why each matters. Classify each as fact to measure, assumption to test, or human concern to understand. End with the one question to investigate first. Avoid a general AI primer.
```

**What the learner should notice:** A vague topic becomes a short, actionable learning agenda.

## Now let them try

Pick your own intimidating subject and research only the highest-value question.

**Evidence of learning:** Questions would change a decision, not merely fill an encyclopedia.

**Integrity guardrail:** Don't let a convenient AI-generated question list replace talking to actual affected people.
