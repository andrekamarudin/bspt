# Don't produce minutes. Produce decisions.

> **Tell a friend:** A meeting isn't done until the next move is clear.

**Human problem:** Everyone attends a meeting; later nobody agrees what was actually decided.

**Byte-size ProTip:** Separate decisions, proposals, actions and open questions. Mark ownership or deadlines unknown unless explicit.

## The before → after moment

**Safe fictional setup:** Fictional transcript: 'Maya: Let's test with 10 users. Dan: Could do 20. Maya: Fine, 10 first. Dan: I'll draft the invite Tuesday. Budget? Maya: Still awaiting finance.'

**Copy/paste demo prompt** (works in an ordinary text chat):

```text
Parse this fictional meeting extract into four sections: Confirmed decisions, Proposed but not agreed, Actions (owner/date only if explicit), and Unresolved questions. Quote the exact phrase supporting each confirmed decision; flag any ambiguity. Extract:
Maya: Let's test with 10 users. Dan: Could do 20. Maya: Fine, 10 first. Dan: I'll draft the invite Tuesday. Budget? Maya: Still awaiting finance.
```

**What the learner should notice:** AI can make the boundary between agreed and merely discussed visible.

## Now let them try

Give the group one ambiguous conversation and ask them to challenge every inferred decision.

**Evidence of learning:** All confirmed decisions are source-backed and uncertainty isn't smoothed away.

**Integrity guardrail:** A structured summary is not official approval; participants must confirm key decisions.
