# Write the message you've been avoiding

> **Tell a friend:** Clear and kind can coexist.

**Human problem:** You need to tell a colleague their work missed the mark, but fear sounding harsh or vague.

**Byte-size ProTip:** Specify facts, intended relationship and concrete ask; request a respectful draft with no invented motives.

## The before → after moment

**Safe fictional setup:** Fictional case: One report missed the agreed Friday deadline; no reason was given; you need a revised ETA.

**Copy/paste demo prompt** (works in an ordinary text chat):

```text
Draft a respectful private message to a colleague. Facts: the report was due Friday; it was not received; no reason is known. Goal: get a realistic new ETA without blame. Tone: direct, calm, empathetic. 75 words max. Avoid guessing motives, passive aggression, fake warmth, or threats. After the draft, flag any factual assumptions.
```

**What the learner should notice:** Clarity need not sacrifice empathy; intent and constraints improve the draft.

## Now let them try

Rewrite the draft in your actual voice. Read it aloud and remove any statement you cannot verify.

**Evidence of learning:** Recipient would know the specific issue, why it matters and what reply is requested.

**Integrity guardrail:** AI cannot know interpersonal history; use your judgment, especially for sensitive HR matters.
