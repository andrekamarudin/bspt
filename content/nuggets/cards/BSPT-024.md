# Sell the change in three human sentences

> **Tell a friend:** People remember the person, the turning point, and the payoff.

**Human problem:** Your useful idea is described with features, jargon and statistics; the listener doesn't see what it changes for a real human.

**Byte-size ProTip:** Compress the pitch to Person → Pivot → Payoff. Keep an authentic human stake, a credible mechanism and a small ask.

## The before → after moment

**Safe fictional setup:** Fictional offer: short training helps busy staff practice one checkable AI workflow. No measured outcomes yet.

**Copy/paste demo prompt** (no private data or paid integration required):

```text
Turn this fictional offer into an ethical 3-line elevator pitch using PERSON → PIVOT → PAYOFF: 'A short workshop for busy staff to practice building one AI-assisted workflow they can verify.' Emphasize confidence and real agency rather than job-loss fear. Do not invent testimonials, results or performance figures. End with one low-pressure invitation. Then explain which line carries empathy, credibility and action.
```

**What the learner should notice:** An abstract 'AI training workshop' becomes a specific transformation someone can imagine themselves making.

## Now let them try

Write your own 3-P pitch for a nonconfidential idea, read it aloud in 20 seconds, and ask a partner what they remember.

**Evidence of learning:** Listener can say who benefits, what changes and what step to take; no unsupported promise.

**Integrity guardrail:** Emotional clarity isn't proof of product-market fit. Validate demand separately.
