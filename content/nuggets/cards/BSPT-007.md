# Turn your brain dump into an executable plan

> **Tell a friend:** You can think messily and still act clearly.

**Human problem:** You have a dozen scattered worries about an upcoming event; organizing them feels harder than doing the work.

**Byte-size ProTip:** Use AI to extract goals, decisions, next actions and blockers from messy notes. Do not let it quietly turn guesses into commitments.

## The before → after moment

**Safe fictional setup:** Fictional notes: 'Need venue? Sarah waiting on signoff; 30 attendees maybe; slides? Ask finance about $500; room booked? send invite by Fri.'

**Copy/paste demo prompt** (works in an ordinary text chat):

```text
Turn these rough notes into a one-page action plan. Group into: goal, decisions required, next actions (owner only if explicit), open questions, and risks. Mark missing owners or dates 'TBD'; do not invent commitments. Highlight the first action that unblocks the most others.
NOTES:
Need venue? Sarah waiting on signoff; 30 attendees maybe; slides? Ask finance about $500; room booked? send invite by Fri.
```

**What the learner should notice:** Unstructured fragments become an accountable plan with uncertainties intact.

## Now let them try

Paste a harmless, anonymized brain dump. Cross out any hallucinated owner or deadline.

**Evidence of learning:** Every action traces to a note or is explicitly labeled a suggestion.

**Integrity guardrail:** Never paste proprietary details or personal data into an unapproved AI service.
