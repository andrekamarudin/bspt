# Turn a wall of policy into a usable checklist

> **Tell a friend:** A good process is one people can actually follow.

**Human problem:** A three-page procedure exists but colleagues keep missing critical steps under pressure.

**Byte-size ProTip:** Extract ordered actions, decision branches, stop conditions and a final verification check without inventing policy.

## The before → after moment

**Safe fictional setup:** Synthetic SOP: 'Before posting, reviewer must approve. If approval missing, hold publication. After publishing, record URL and timestamp.'

**Copy/paste demo prompt** (works in an ordinary text chat):

```text
Convert this exact fictional SOP into a checklist with the same requirements and no new obligations. Include: prerequisites, steps in order, a STOP condition, and a final verification box. Cite the SOP sentence behind each item. SOP: 'Before posting, reviewer must approve. If approval missing, hold publication. After publishing, record URL and timestamp.'
```

**What the learner should notice:** The AI can transform presentation, but the approval condition must survive word-for-word in meaning.

## Now let them try

Check the output for a deliberately omitted stop condition, then add it back and explain why it matters.

**Evidence of learning:** Every MUST and STOP condition is preserved and source-mapped.

**Integrity guardrail:** Use a qualified reviewer for regulated procedures; never silently change binding requirements.
